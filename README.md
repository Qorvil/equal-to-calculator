### Project Description
Equal To Calculator is a gui-based calculator that takes a ridiculous amount of time to find even the most simple solutions. 

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Clearly, humans are the top species in almost all aspects. We dominate the food chain, live the most comfortable lives, and create beautiful art. But the one thing humans are not so good at is calculation, which computers are great at. Obviously, this creates a disparity between humans and computers which can be a source of inequality between us and our creation.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
Our calculator, the Equal To Calculator, aims to reduce this inequality by being bad at calculations - it makes absurd mistakes, take a long time, and makes confident answers that are complete bonkers. In a way, the Equal To Calculator is a calculator that is truly "Equal To Humans." 

## Technical Details
### Technologies/Components Used
For Software:
- [Languages used] - Python, HTML, JavaScript, CSS

- [Libraries used] - SymPy, Flask, math, random, re

### Implementation
For Software:
Flask and SymPy privately calculate equations, while a custom engine generates convoluted steps steering toward a believable wrong answer. The vanilla JavaScript frontend animates these steps, blurs the final result behind an interactive canvas CAPTCHA, provides a sticky Subway Surfers side panel, and triggers a humorous donation QR pop-up modal.
